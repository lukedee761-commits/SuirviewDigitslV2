One-time build, optional monthly care

At SuirViewDigital your build is a one-time cost, and a monthly plan afterwards is optional. Both cover the parts people forget about — hosting, security, updates and someone to call:

  • Pay once to build it: a single one-time cost for the build — from €349 for a Starter site — and you own it outright, with hosting and your domain included for the first year.
  • Optional monthly care: from €50/month, a Care plan keeps everything hosted, secure and updated; Growth and Pro add fresh content and SEO to help you climb.

When paying once makes sense

The one-time route is usually the cheapest over the long run — there's no recurring fee, so once it's paid, it's paid. It suits owners who have the budget available upfront and like the idea of owning the finished site outright. It also pairs neatly with the Trading Online Voucher grant, which can offset a big chunk of the cost.

When a monthly plan makes sense

A monthly Care plan suits owners who want the site looked after without thinking about it, or who want fresh content and SEO working over time. From €50 a month it covers the upkeep, so the site keeps growing rather than standing still. It's the lower-commitment way in — ideal if you're just getting started or protecting cashflow.

“There's no catch in either plan — the difference is just cashflow. Some people want to pay once and own it; others want it off their plate for a set monthly cost. I'll tell you honestly which one actually suits your situation, not which one's better for me.” — Luke Stapleton, SuirViewDigital

What's included either way

Whichever you choose, the essentials are covered: secure hosting, an SSL certificate, ongoing maintenance, and changes handled for you — plus a guarantee that if anything ever breaks, it's repaired within 24 hours. No surprise bills, and a real person answers the phone. You can see the packages and monthly plans on the Plans section.

So which should you choose?

A simple rule of thumb: if you have the budget upfront and want the lowest long-term cost, pay once. If you'd rather protect cashflow and keep it fully hands-off, go monthly. Still unsure? Frequently asked questions

Is it cheaper to pay once or monthly?

You pay once for the build either way — that is the main cost. A monthly Care plan (from €50) is optional and just covers upkeep, so there is no cheaper plan to pick, only whether you want it managed.

Do I own the website if I pay monthly?

You always own your domain. You pay once to build the site and own it outright; an optional monthly plan just looks after it for you while you're subscribed.

Can a grant help pay for a website in Ireland?

Yes — the Trading Online Voucher can cover up to €2,500, which suits the one-time option especially well. See localenterprise.ie.
